THE TANK IS EMPTY.
NO FISH HAVE BEEN HATCHED YET.

Dear Jireh,

Oh my dearest Jireh, I write to you as a man lost at war, writing his final goodbyes within the trenches of Normandy. Instead I am stuck at home, a mere 30km distance away. How could I possibly close the distance?

Well... When forseeing what you've been making for me, I thought about how I could possibly match that level of ingenuinty and sincerity, whilst writing for an upcoming prose, I cam across this idea: A website which stores all of my essays/poems in an organized place.

Welcome to Enzo's aquarium, and have fun reading :)

- Love, Gabriel
